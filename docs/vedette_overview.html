GHFC · Institut Pasteur — germline CNV caller · Version française

Vedette

A copy-number variant caller (DEL/DUP) for Illumina WGS, from 1 to 10,000 genomes, built around one conviction: the CNV problem is not detecting them, it is sorting the real ones from the false ones.

§At a glance

  • Input: aligned CRAMs (bwa-mem2) — and, when available, precomputed 1 kb depth bedgraphs (GHFC fast path).
  • Output: per-sample VCF, then multi-sample cohort VCF (GT/GQ/SQ/CN), HTML report, per-family workspaces.
  • Differentiator: a three-tier post-calling filter, including a calibrated score (SQ) learned from GIAB truth and GHFC trios — calling stays deliberately permissive, specificity is the filter's job.
  • Scale: validated on 2,670 genomes (665 trios); cohort re-genotyping without ever re-reading a CRAM; a full genome re-filtered in ~14 s after a model update.

1The evidence channels

A single pass over the CRAM feeds six channels, stored once and for all:

Read depth (RD)

binned (100 bp or 1 kb), GC-corrected by strata, N/exclusion masks, normalized to the diploid autosomal median.

Discordant pairs (PE)

+/− orientation with a long insert = DEL; everted −/+ pairs = tandem DUP.

Split reads (SR) + hidden splits

clips ≥20 bp are realigned locally (fit alignment): the realignment geometry yields base-pair-precise junctions — and recovers tandem DUPs smaller than the insert size, the classic blind spot.

CIGAR deletions

D operations ≥30 bp carried by the reads themselves, merged per read.

Insert-size shift

binned track of concordant-pair insert sizes: a run of shifted bins betrays a small DEL without a clean junction.

BAF

allele fractions at heterozygous SNVs — in the HMM emissions and in the genotype likelihood.

On top of this, a sample QC computed in passing: the wave score (fraction of spectral energy at periods ≥200 kb of the corrected ratio), which flags libraries with oscillating coverage — 5× slower to process and prolific producers of false calls.

2The evidence store

All the evidence for one genome fits in a versioned Zarr store (~91 MB): it is the contract between stages. The filter, cohort re-genotyping and trio analyses re-read this store — never the CRAM. This is what makes it possible to re-genotype 2,670 samples at 500,000 sites in a few hours, or to fully re-filter after a model update in one hour of cluster time.

3The pipeline

What call does

A CN-state HMM {0…5} segments the depth ratio (ratio-Gaussian emissions + beta mixture on the BAF); in parallel, PE/SR/CIGAR junctions are clustered and clips are realigned. Segments and junctions are reconciled, then each candidate is genotyped by the unified likelihood: depth (with sub-bin dilution), junctions (negative binomial on read counts) and BAF. This is methodological claim #1: one likelihood for all evidence types, where existing callers use BAF (at best) post hoc.

4The four-tier filter

  • Tier 1 — transparent hard filters: minimum size, N/exclusion mask overlap, segdup without SR support, insufficient informative bins. Every tag is readable in the VCF FILTER column.
  • Tier 2 — calibrated SQ score: two gradient-boosting models (DEL and DUP) over 30 features (depth, junctions, BAF, genomic context, wave QC), monotonic constraints, chromosome-held-out cross-validation, isotonic calibration — SQ is a phred of P(true). Trained on GIAB HG002 v5.0q truth, HG003/HG004 transmission semi-supervision, and since v5 on wavy GHFC trios labeled by parental transmission. Current operating threshold: SQ ≥ 5 ≈ 0.95 precision.
  • Tier 3 — cohort: inflation-free AF (bounded-centroid clustering, never transitive chaining), exact HWE, recurrent artifacts (CohortArtifact), and recurrence rescue: a site below threshold in every carrier but recurrent (≥2 carriers, SQ sum ≥ 8) is rescued and traced RESCUE=RECURRENT — this is what recovered a familial SHANK3 deletion present in 7 relatives at SQ ~2 each.
  • Tier 4 — cross-cohort common-variant flags (vedette cohort-filter): HighCarrierFrac (carried by ≥85% of genotyped samples — reference/mappability artifacts HWE cannot see, since "everyone 1/1" is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium), CNDiscordant (the re-genotyped copy number of most carriers contradicts the SV type — a "DUP" sitting at CN=1), and, with --known-sites <reference cohort.vcf.gz>, CommonRef (matches a site carried by ≥50% of a larger reference cohort). Added 2026-09-24 after manual IGV review of a 9-member family run: 10 of 13 reviewed PASS calls were false, every one sharing this exact signature (8–9/9 local carriers, 46–100% frequency in a 2,670-genome reference) — a small family run has no frequency of its own to catch it.
Two QC guardrails complete the triage: wavy samples (score ≥ 0.145) are flagged in the report, and a child carrying more than 20 PASS de novo candidates is marked as a technical outlier — a real genome carries fewer than one.

5Manual validation loop (IGV)

vedette report --family-events writes one interactive page per family workspace (families/<FID>/events.html): every DEL/DUP the family carries, with chromosome/type/validation filters, click-to-sort columns, an inheritance column (de novo / maternal / paternal / biparental, crossed from the Mendel report), the Tier-4 context columns above (SQmax, run carrier fraction, reference-cohort frequency) and per-member GT:GQ:CN.

Each row carries an IGV link — jumps a running IGV desktop to the locus (±20% flanks) via its batch-command port — and three exclusive checkboxes, vrai / faux / incertain, for a manual verdict (a forced true/false choice pollutes labels on genuinely ambiguous calls: borderline coverage, a fuzzy breakpoint, a repeat region). Verdicts live in the browser's local storage and export as a TSV, re-importable and matched by site id, then locus, then ≥90% reciprocal overlap — so a re-merged cohort's renumbered sites still line up. This loop produced the Tier 4 flags above and feeds the next filter model's training labels.

6Usage

One sample

# calling (CRAM + bedgraph fast path) then filtering with the current model
vedette call --sample S1 --out-dir out/ --config configs/ghfc.toml \
    --cram S1.cram --depth-bedgraph S1.by1000.bedgraph.gz --vaf-bedgraph S1.vaf.bedgraph.gz
vedette filter out/S1.vedette.vcf.gz --store out/S1.evidence.zarr \
    --out out/S1.filtered.vcf.gz --config configs/ghfc.toml --model-dir models/v6

A cohort (Nextflow + SLURM)

./launch_driver.sh --manifest cohort.manifest.tsv --pedigree cohort.pedigree.tsv \
    --outdir runs/cohort --model_dir models/v6 \
    --steps call,filter,merge,genotype,cohort,ped,report -with-report -resume

The manifest is generated from the GHFC directory layout with vedette manifest discover; vedette report produces the HTML page (KPIs, size spectra, Mendel sweep, family sections with genes and NDD impact score) and the families/<FID>/ workspaces.

8Known limits & next steps

  • Small-DEL recall (50–300 bp) plateaus at ~30% in short-read — like the state of the art.
  • Inversions and translocations (v2): the INV/INTER PE evidence is already stored, not yet called.
  • Recurrence rescue is deliberately permissive at scale — filterable in one stroke on RESCUE; a cohort-size-adaptive criterion is planned.
  • PyPI/bioconda release: prepared (container, CI, CITATION.cff), triggered only on the team's decision.
  • The transmission-based semi-supervision labels (v5, wavy GHFC trios) turn out to be contaminated by shared familial artifacts — a real fix is cleaning them with the Tier-4 reference frequency before the next training round (v6).
